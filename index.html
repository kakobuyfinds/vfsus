Sizing and QC photo checklist

Save the product URL, size, color and date you checked the listing so you can match the warehouse photos to your order.

Shoes and slides

Check whether the size chart measures the foot or the insole. For QC, ask for both shoes side by side, a clear size label and a view of the outsoles.

Tees and sweatshirts

Compare flat chest width and body length with a garment you own. Ask for a close-up of embroidery or print placement when it matters to your choice.

Jackets

Compare shoulder width and sleeve length over the layers you plan to wear. Ask for photos of the closures, lining and size label.

Estimate your landed cost

Convert each quoted amount to USD in advance. For shipping, use the parcel quote for your destination and check whether it is based on actual or dimensional weight.

Enter a quoted amount to start.

Include domestic shipping in the shipping field. Leave unknown charges blank and treat the displayed amount as a subtotal until all charges are known.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping shortlist for people considering HooBuy as a buying agent. A useful row keeps the item link, exact variant, reference price and any measurements or QC notes together.

Where do the listed products come from?

The prices and names come from the CAFANS featured catalog. Product links open the corresponding CAFANS detail pages. The two main buttons open the CAFANS homepage.

What if the listing or variant has changed?

Recheck the listing title, item identifier and photos against your shortlist. If the exact option has disappeared, ask the seller or agent to confirm availability before choosing a replacement.
